# Section variants: which won and why

(100 - 30 per FAIL - 6 per WARN) and keeps the best; all are in local/wip/<session>/<level>.variants/ (index.json, whole-level files s<i>_A/B/C: the viewer's format). A CLOSE
result is a taste call for the user; the user's own picks count double (write them here as "user picked").
