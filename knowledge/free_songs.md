# Free songs: Newgrounds songs we may publish

The songs here have a **Creative Commons** license on their Newgrounds page, so we may put them in git and play them on
the public site (https://evgiz0r.github.io/GDLevelMaker/) next to a level, with a credit line. They also work in GD: every
one was fetched through GD's own song server (`tools/songs.js`). The data is `knowledge/free_songs.json` (tools read it);
this page is the readable copy. Checked 2026-11-25, song by song, from each page's "Licensing Terms".

**Rules that come with them:**
- Credit the artist (`credit` in the json; the public viewer shows it under the player, linked to the Newgrounds page).
- NC = non-commercial only (the site is free and has no ads: fine). ND (Two Moons) = no edits of the audio: no cuts,
  no speed-ups; the level may start at an offset (that plays it, does not change it).
- SA = share-alike, only matters if we change the song itself (we don't).
- A song's file goes in `songs/free/<id>.mp3` only when a public level uses it (keeps the repo small); copy it from the
  PC's `local/songs/<id>.mp3`. `node tests/pages_build.js` fails on a file in songs/free/ that is not on this list.
- Licenses can change: re-check a song's page before its first publish.
