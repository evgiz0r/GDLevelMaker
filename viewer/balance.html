How the number is decided

score = log10(expected attempts) = Σ over presses of −log10 p. Every press must work in one run, so the chances multiply; the log turns that product into a sum, and each press adds its own cost. 1.0 means about 10 attempts, 2.0 about 100, 3.0 about 1000.

1. The route a person playsThe solver's route, turned into taps (one per jump or orb), and where a tight press has an easier way (an orb instead of a frame-perfect jump), that way.
2. A window per pressEach press is moved earlier and later and replayed; the window is how far it can move and the run still lives (up to 200 ms each way).
3. A chance per pressp = (1 − LAPSE) · erf(window/2 / (SIGMA·√2)). SIGMA is your timing spread, LAPSE the chance to fluff any press. Both were fitted on your own recorded runs.
4. Settling after a portalA press whose window closes under SETTLE ms after a mode, speed or size portal has its chance scaled down by the time left, since you have not settled into the new mode yet.
